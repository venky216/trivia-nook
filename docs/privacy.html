Privacy Policy for Trivia Nook

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how the Trivia Nook mobile app ("the app"), published by

the Trivia Nook developer ("we"), handles information. If you have questions,

contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your profile name, chosen avatar, quiz progress, statistics, coins, badges and

settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that

receive this information, and we do not require an account. You can delete

it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To

deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US

states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent

tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any

time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region).

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove ads" or coin packs) are processed

by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google

Play's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy

policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Games (if enabled)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games to use global leaderboards,

your Play Games profile name and scores are processed by Google under its

privacy policy.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not

directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal

information from children.

Links

The app may open links (for example, this policy or the store page) in your

browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this

address with a new "Last updated" date.
