Privacy Policy for GK Trivia: General Knowledge

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how the GK Trivia: General Knowledge mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your profile name, chosen avatar, quiz progress, statistics, coins, badges and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information, and we do not require an account. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and plans them on your device. They are created by the app itself: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and no push-notification service is used. You can turn the reminder off at any time in Settings → Daily reminder, or in your device's notification settings.

Friend challenges

When you create a friend challenge, the app makes a short code that holds only which questions were asked, whether each of your answers was right and how quickly you answered, and which avatar picture you use. It does not contain your name or anything about your device. The code goes only where you choose to send it; we never receive it.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove ads" or coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app uses it to:

  • save a backup of your quiz progress (level, XP, coins, statistics, badges and which questions you've seen) in your Google Play Games account, so you can restore it on another device; and
  • submit your scores to global leaderboards, shown with your Play Games profile name.

This data is stored by Google, not by us, under Google's privacy policy. You can stop at any time by signing out of Play Games, and delete saved games and Play Games data from your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com/ → Data & privacy, or in the Google Play Games settings.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links

The app may open links (for example, this policy or the store page) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
