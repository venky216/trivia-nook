Privacy Policy for GK Trivia: General Knowledge

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how the GK Trivia: General Knowledge mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information on Android and iPhone. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your profile name, chosen avatar, quiz progress, statistics, coins, badges and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information (only the anonymous usage statistics described below are sent), and we do not require an account. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To learn which parts of the app people use and to fix problems, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. They receive anonymous information such as which screens are opened, the kind of action taken (for example "a Journey quiz was started", "a quiz was finished with 8 of 10 right", "the shop was opened"), the app version, device model and operating system, an app-instance identifier created by Firebase, approximate country (from the IP address), and crash logs. They never receive your profile name, avatar, friend-challenge codes or anything else you type, and they do not use your advertising ID. This data is not used for advertising. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Share usage stats. Google keeps this data according to its own policies (Firebase privacy).

On iPhone, Firebase may also use the identifier Apple gives each app maker (the "identifier for vendor") to count app installs. Firebase does not use your advertising identifier.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and plans them on your device. They are created by the app itself: nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and no push-notification service is used. You can turn the reminder off at any time in Settings → Daily reminder, or in your device's notification settings.

Friend challenges

When you create a friend challenge, the app makes a short code that holds only which questions were asked, whether each of your answers was right and how quickly you answered, and which avatar picture you use. It does not contain your name or anything about your device. The code goes only where you choose to send it; we never receive it.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: short full-screen ads between some quizzes (never during a question), and video ads you choose to watch for coins. On Android a small banner may also appear; the iPhone app shows no banners. Buying "Remove ads" (GK Trivia Pro) stops all ads except the video ads you choose to watch. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking, see below),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

Your profile name, avatar, answers and progress are never shared with Google or advertisers.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region).

Tracking on iPhone

On iPhone, before any ad is shown, the app explains why it asks and then shows Apple's "Allow tracking?" prompt (App Tracking Transparency). Only if you tap Allow can Google use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and measure them across other companies' apps and websites. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the app still works fully and still shows ads, but the advertising identifier is not available and the ads are not based on tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases ("Remove ads" / GK Trivia Pro, and coin packs) are processed by Google Play on Android or the App Store on iPhone. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy, and Apple's in Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using the rating window built into Google Play or the App Store. Your rating goes to the store, not to us.

Sign-in, leaderboards and cloud save

The app does not use Google Play Games, Game Center or any other sign-in, online leaderboard or cloud save at present; the Ranks tab is worked out on your device. If a future version adds one, this policy will be updated first.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links

The app may open links (for example, this policy or the store page) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
